Plan Delivery Comparator

Advanced Plan Delivery Simulator

For Educational and Research Purpose Only. Not For Clinical Use.

  • Load: Drag/drop a DICOM RT Plan (.dcm) or exported JSON, or use Choose File.
  • Limits: Set machine speed/accel limits globally, then click Apply & Broadcast.
  • Play: Select a beam or enable Simulate Full Plan, then Play/Pause. Beep per MU is optional.
  • Scrub: Use the CP bar + slider or hover radial plots/mini stamps to jump between CPs.
  • Views: BEV shows interpolated MLC/jaws; crosshair ticks are 1 cm. Radial/XY plots track the current CP.
Load one or more RT Plans (RP):
Drag & drop or pick files; one embedded simulator column per RP will appear.
